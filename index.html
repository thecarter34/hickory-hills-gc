The Course

An executive course built for the recreational golfer.

Eighteen well-manicured holes, walkable in two hours or less.

Just three miles south of Eau Claire on Highway 93, Hickory Hills threads through rolling woodlands, around the water that gives the front nine its name, and into some of the most forgiving greens in the Chippewa Valley. It's the kind of course where you can break 90 on a Tuesday afternoon — or simply enjoy the walk.

Par 63 across all 18 holes. Slope 60.5 / Rating 96. Soft-spike facility · shirts & shoes required at all times.

  • Par 63 · executive length
  • 3 tee sets (Blue / White / Red)
  • Cart & pull-cart rental
  • Pro shop & practice green
  • Banquet facilities on-site

Junior Programs

Building the next generation of Chippewa Valley golfers.

In partnership with Eau Claire Parks & Recreation.

Weekday lessons cover stance, grip, alignment, posture, aim, balance and swing mechanics. The Junior Golf League adds supervised on-course play each Friday morning — kids that complete lessons play for $1 per round all season.

Lessons: Ages 6–7

8:30 – 9:30 AM

Foundations of stance, grip and aim. Clubs provided.

Lessons: Ages 8–9

9:45 – 10:45 AM

Building consistency and course etiquette.

Lessons: Ages 10+

11:00 AM – 12:15 PM

Full swing mechanics and short-game skills.

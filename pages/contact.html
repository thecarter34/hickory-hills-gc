Driving Directions

Three miles south of Eau Claire on Highway 93.

From the North

Highway 93 southbound

Follow Highway 93 south from the Hwy 93 & Hwy 94 interchange for 4 miles. Course on your left.

From the South

Highway 93 northbound

Follow Highway 93 north from Hwy 10 for 14 miles. Course on your right.

From the East

Highway 53 south to Hickory Rd

Follow Hwy 53 south to Hickory Road. Turn right on Hickory Rd, then 4 miles. Course on your right.

An 18-hole executive course in the heart of the Chippewa Valley.

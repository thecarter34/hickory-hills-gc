Home/Junior Programs

Building the next generation of Chippewa Valley golfers.

In partnership with Eau Claire Parks & Recreation — weekday lessons and a Friday Junior League, ages 6–15.

Lessons · Mon–Thu

Weekday Junior Lessons

Classes cover stance, grip, alignment, posture, aim, balance, proper swing techniques, plus basic golf course rules and etiquette.

Ages 6–7

8:30 – 9:30 AM

Foundations of stance, grip, and aim. Clubs provided. Comfortable clothing required.

Ages 8–9

9:45 – 10:45 AM

Building consistency and introducing course etiquette and short-game basics.

Ages 10+

11:00 AM – 12:15 PM

Full swing mechanics, course management, and short-game skills.

Kids who complete a session golf for $1/round with a paying adult all season. All jewelry off, water bottle filled. Programs modify for high heat — not cancelled.

On the Course · Friday Mornings

2026 Junior Golf League

Ages 7–15. Short lesson, then 9 holes with supervision. The purpose is education, skill development, and fun with age-appropriate competition.

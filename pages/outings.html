Bring your group. We'll handle the setup.

Charity scrambles, corporate days, wedding send-offs, retirement parties, and family tournaments. Banquet facilities on site for post-round gatherings.

What we help with

Outing coordination, from cart staging to the final toast.

Every outing is coordinated on-site by Megan Bechel or General Manager John Norby.

  • Cart staging & closest-to-pin markers
  • Scorecards & rules sheets for the field
  • Boxed lunches or banquet dinner coordination
  • Skins, longest drive, hole-in-one prizes
  • On-course beverage service
  • Banquet facilities for post-round gatherings

Outings typically run 16–144 players. Prime summer dates fill by mid-spring. Have your dates in mind before you call.
